Pandosy Village

Walkable, Vibrant & Sophisticated

The Pandosy corridor is one of Kelowna's most walkable and sought-after areas. Boutique shops, farm-to-table restaurants, Okanagan College, and easy access to the beach create a sophisticated urban village atmosphere. A favourite for young professionals, students, and those seeking a connected lifestyle close to the lake.
