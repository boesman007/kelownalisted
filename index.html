About Nick

From South Africa

Nick Van Rensburg's path to Kelowna is anything but ordinary. Born and raised in South Africa, Nick built his real estate career across two continents — sharpening his skills in property development in the UK before calling the Okanagan home in 2021.

With over 20 years of international real estate experience, Nick combines the reach of Real Broker's cutting-edge platform with the personal touch of a local expert who genuinely cares about your outcome.
